How these are counted
Each vacancy counts once, in the period its JD was first sent to review. Call coverage is how much of what the client said in the Intro Call and the Deep Dive made it into that first JD: core points weigh double, partial mentions count half, and each point the JD states differently or each requirement nobody asked for takes 5 more off (up to 20 each). The number is computed from the AI's point-by-point check, not picked by the AI. Rates are over JDs you already decided. Checklist items are not exclusive, so they add up to more than 100.
