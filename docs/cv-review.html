How these are counted
A profile is one candidate for one vacancy, counted in the period it was first sent. Rates are over profiles you already decided, so a backlog doesn't flatter anyone. JD coverage is the share of the vacancy's technical requirements the first-round CV shows — later rounds are coverage after your coaching. It measures the match between candidate and opening, not how well the recruiter writes, and it only counts analyses scored the current way. Rejection reasons are not exclusive, so their percentages add up to more than 100. Needs changes counts as decided, like the other two: the round is closed and the ball is back with the recruiter. It is kept apart from rejected on purpose — a rejection is about the candidate, a correction is about the document, and only the rejection marks the candidate in the batch.
